# Research Brief: The Market Research Agency of 2030

- **Brief name:** future-of-research-2030
- **Research mode:** systematic-review
- **Commissioning organisation:** Fifth Quadrant
- **Date:** April 2026
- **Prepared by:** Steve Nuttall, Director

---

## Background and Context

Fifth Quadrant is a B2B market research agency headquartered in Sydney, Australia. We combine innovative research methodologies, proven strategic frameworks, and AI-driven tools to help enterprise clients act with confidence on what matters tomorrow. Our tagline is "creating tomorrow today".

Artificial intelligence is reshaping how data is collected, analysed, and interpreted across the research industry. We need a rigorous, evidence-based picture of what the market research agency looks like in 2030 to make well-grounded investment decisions now. The findings will directly inform our decisions on AI investment, new product development, talent strategy, competitive positioning, and competitive moat building.

---

## Research Questions

**Primary:** What does the market research agency look like in 2030, and what must a mid-size B2B specialist agency do now to remain competitive in that environment?

**Subsidiary:**
- SQ1: Which elements of the traditional agency value chain are most at risk of automation or disintermediation by AI tools and self-serve platforms, and at what pace?
- SQ2: What will B2B clients value from an agency in 2030 that they cannot obtain from AI-native platforms or in-house capability?
- SQ3: Which agency models (boutique specialist, tech-enabled hybrid, full-service, platform-led) are best positioned to survive and grow?
- SQ4: What skills will research professionals need in 2030, and how are universities, professional bodies (ESOMAR, AMSRO, MRS), and agencies responding?
- SQ5: In adjacent industries where AI disruption is further advanced (legal research, management consulting, financial analysis, journalism), what lessons apply directly to market research?

**Exclusions:** Anonymous or unverifiable authors; vendor marketing without independent evidence; sources not accessible in full text

## Scenario Analysis

**Required:** Yes

**Key scenario drivers:**
- Pace of AI capability development relative to quality thresholds for commodity research (NPS, brand tracking, satisfaction)
- Rate of client adoption of AI-native self-serve research tools versus retained agency relationships
- Whether regulatory and governance requirements favour established agencies over platforms
- Speed of hybrid AI-skills pipeline development relative to demand

---

## Strategic Application

These findings will directly inform four investment decisions at Fifth Quadrant:

- **AI Capability and Tools:** Which AI capabilities to prioritise in the next 12 to 24 months
- **New Products and Services:** What new research products or service models to develop or acquire
- **Talent and Skills:** What skills profile to recruit for; which roles are most at risk; what training is needed
- **Positioning and Differentiation:** How to position relative to AI-native platforms and large full-service agencies

**Competitive moat question:** What durable competitive advantages could Fifth Quadrant build that would be difficult for rivals to replicate or erode? For each moat: source, replication timeline, most plausible breach scenario, and investments that would deepen rather than merely maintain it. Flag which moats are durable across all three scenarios versus scenario-dependent.
