---

## Background and Context

Valvoline Global Operations is seeking to enter the Australian power generation lubricants market. This desk research phase (Phase 1 of the research programme) will provide the market intelligence needed to inform Valvoline's market entry strategy, identify commercially viable opportunities, and support the development of a targeted go-to-market approach.

The focus is on businesses and facilities that operate machinery for electricity production, including generators, turbines, and backup power systems, with a qualifying volume threshold of 25,000 to 35,000 litres per month. Product categories include engine oil, hydraulic oil, gear oil, industrial oil, grease, transmission fluid, coolant, and heat transfer fluid.

The desk research feeds a Decision Gate Workshop before Phase 2 qualitative fieldwork commences. It must be comprehensive enough to inform the Phase 2 discussion guide and sample structure.

---

## Research Questions

1. **Market sizing:** What is the addressable market for power generation lubricants in Australia? What is the breakdown by sector (data centres, waste-to-energy, mining, manufacturing co-generation, healthcare and commercial facilities), geography, and application type (prime power versus backup/standby)?

2. **Competitive landscape:** Who are the incumbent suppliers (Mobil, Shell, BP/Castrol, FUCHS, TotalEnergies, Gulf Western)? What is their market positioning, channel approach, OEM relationships, service offering, and estimated market share?

3. **Channel and distribution structure:** How is the market served? What is the role of service contractors, OEM dealers, distributors, and facilities management companies in lubricant purchasing? Who holds the buying decision at each layer?

4. **Decision-makers and procurement:** Who are the key decision-makers in each priority sector? How do they buy? What are the qualifying criteria for supplier selection, the key switching triggers, and the role of OEM specifications and warranty requirements?

5. **Valvoline and Cummins positioning:** What is the strategic value of the Cummins South Pacific partnership for accessing priority segments, particularly data centres and waste-to-energy? What segments are most accessible through this channel versus direct routes?

6. **Market entry implications:** What are the key barriers to entry, the most commercially viable entry points by sector, and the priority accounts or segments for a targeted go-to-market approach?

---

## Scope

- **Geography:** Australia; state-by-state breakdown where data is available (prioritise NSW, VIC, QLD)
- **Sector:** Power generation — data centres, waste-to-energy, mining, manufacturing co-generation, healthcare and commercial facilities
- **Volume threshold:** Facilities operating at 25,000 to 35,000 litres per month
- **Date range for sources:** Last 3 years preferred; older sources acceptable for structural market information
- **Named competitors to profile:** Mobil, Shell, BP/Castrol, FUCHS, TotalEnergies, Gulf Western
- **Named organisations:** Cummins South Pacific, Aggreko Australia (for channel intelligence)
- **Out of scope:** Automotive lubricants, consumer products, markets outside Australia
