# PRISMA 2020 Checklist

Used in `systematic-review` mode. The completed checklist becomes Appendix A of the final report. Reference: Page MJ et al. *BMJ* 2021; 372:n71.

For each item, the report should record: location (which section/page addresses it) and a brief note.

## Title
- [ ] **1. Title.** Identify the report as a systematic review.

## Abstract
- [ ] **2. Abstract.** See PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts checklist (covered by the Executive Summary in our format).

## Introduction
- [ ] **3. Rationale.** Describe the rationale for the review in the context of existing knowledge.
- [ ] **4. Objectives.** Provide an explicit statement of objective(s) or question(s).

## Methods
- [ ] **5. Eligibility criteria.** Specify the inclusion and exclusion criteria and how studies were grouped.
- [ ] **6. Information sources.** Specify all sources searched (databases, registers, websites, organisations, contact with study authors) and the date each was last searched.
- [ ] **7. Search strategy.** Present the full search strategies for all databases, registers, and websites.
- [ ] **8. Selection process.** Specify the methods used to decide whether a study met inclusion criteria, including how many reviewers screened each record and study, whether they worked independently, and any automation tools used.
- [ ] **9. Data collection process.** Specify the methods used to collect data from reports, including how many reviewers collected data, whether they worked independently, processes for obtaining or confirming data, and any automation tools used.
- [ ] **10a. Data items — outcomes.** List and define all outcomes for which data were sought.
- [ ] **10b. Data items — other variables.** List and define all other variables for which data were sought (e.g. participant and intervention characteristics, funding sources).
- [ ] **11. Study risk of bias assessment.** Specify the methods used to assess risk of bias in the included studies.
- [ ] **12. Effect measures.** Specify the effect measure(s) used in the synthesis or presentation of results.
- [ ] **13a–f. Synthesis methods.** Describe the processes used to decide which studies were eligible for each synthesis; data preparation; tabulation/visualisation methods; synthesis methods including statistical methods; methods for exploring heterogeneity; sensitivity analyses.
- [ ] **14. Reporting bias assessment.** Describe any methods used to assess risk of bias due to missing results.
- [ ] **15. Certainty assessment.** Describe any methods used to assess certainty (or confidence) in the body of evidence.

## Results
- [ ] **16a. Study selection — flow.** Describe the results of the search and selection process, ideally using a flow diagram.
- [ ] **16b. Study selection — exclusions.** Cite studies that might appear to meet inclusion criteria but were excluded, and explain why.
- [ ] **17. Study characteristics.** Cite each included study and present its characteristics.
- [ ] **18. Risk of bias in studies.** Present assessments of risk of bias for each included study.
- [ ] **19. Results of individual studies.** For all outcomes, present for each study: summary statistics for each group and an effect estimate with precision (e.g. confidence/credible interval).
- [ ] **20a–d. Results of syntheses.** Present results of syntheses, statistical heterogeneity, sub-group/sensitivity analyses.
- [ ] **21. Reporting biases.** Present assessments of risk of bias due to missing results for each synthesis.
- [ ] **22. Certainty of evidence.** Present assessments of certainty in the body of evidence for each outcome.

## Discussion
- [ ] **23a. Interpretation — general.** Provide a general interpretation of the results in the context of other evidence.
- [ ] **23b. Interpretation — limitations of evidence.** Discuss any limitations of the evidence included in the review.
- [ ] **23c. Interpretation — limitations of review.** Discuss any limitations of the review processes used.
- [ ] **23d. Interpretation — implications.** Discuss implications of the results for practice, policy, and future research.

## Other information
- [ ] **24a. Registration and protocol — registration.** Provide registration information for the review, including the register name and registration number, or state that the review was not registered.
- [ ] **24b. Registration and protocol — access.** Indicate where the review protocol can be accessed, or state that a protocol was not prepared.
- [ ] **24c. Registration and protocol — amendments.** Describe any amendments to information provided at registration or in the protocol.
- [ ] **25. Support.** Describe sources of financial or non-financial support for the review, and the role of the funders/sponsors.
- [ ] **26. Competing interests.** Declare any competing interests of review authors.
- [ ] **27. Availability of data, code, and other materials.** Report which of the following are publicly available and where they can be found: template data collection forms; data extracted from included studies; data used for analyses; analytic code; any other materials used in the review.

---

**Note for FQ briefs:** For most strategic intelligence reviews (e.g. The Future of Research 2030), several PRISMA items are answered "not applicable" — particularly statistical synthesis items (12, 13c–f, 19, 20a–d, 21, 22) and registration items (24a–c). State "Not applicable — narrative thematic synthesis, not a quantitative meta-analysis" rather than leaving items blank.
